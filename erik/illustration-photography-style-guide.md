## PART 1: ILLUSTRATION STYLE (For App UI Characters)

### Brand Character Profile

**Name:** Adora (working title - you can name her)
**Personality:**
- Warm, approachable, non-judgmental
- Confident but not arrogant
- Curious, encouraging, celebratory
- Culturally neutral, body-neutral

**Visual Characteristics:**
- Age: Appears 25-35 (ageless, timeless appeal)
- Build: Slightly rounded, soft forms
- Body type: Medium/curvy (relatable, not extreme)
- Skin tone: Warm, medium depth (can be customized for different markets)
- Expression: Warm smile, encouraging eyes, approachable

**Style Keywords:**
- Flat + soft shadows (depth without realism)
- Rounded corners everywhere (gentle aesthetic)
- Warm color palette (pastels + warm neutrals)
- Friendly but sophisticated (not cartoonish)
- Body-positive (curves celebrated, not hidden)
- Accessibility-first (clear, readable, not overly stylized)

### Reference Visual Style Examples

**EXCELLENT MATCHES (Study these):**

1. **Bumble (Dating App)**
   - Warm, feminine character design
   - Rounded forms, soft colors
   - Empowering messaging
   - Body-positive approach
   - Style: Modern illustration with personality

2. **Headspace (Meditation App)**
   - Calm, approachable characters
   - Minimalist + soft shadows
   - Diverse representation
   - Soothing aesthetics
   - Style: Flat illustration with subtle depth

3. **Calm (Sleep App)**
   - Warm, inviting character art
   - Soft color palettes
   - Nature-inspired (but not too realistic)
   - Meditative feeling
   - Style: Illustration with watercolor influence

4. **Coolors.co (Color Tool)**
   - Playful character design
   - Pastels + warm colors
   - Rounded, friendly forms
   - Accessible, inclusive characters
   - Style: Flat design with character personality

5. **Duolingo (Language App)**
   - Character-driven branding
   - Warm, encouraging approach
   - Diverse character designs
   - Celebratory moments
   - Style: Stylized illustration, cheerful

**STYLE NOT TO MATCH:**

- ✗ Hyper-realistic body drawings (creates body scrutiny)
- ✗ Anime/manga style (too niche, cultural specificity)
- ✗ Minimalist line art (not warm enough for empowerment)
- ✗ Corporate/stock illustration (lacks personality)
- ✗ Overly stylized/artistic (accessibility challenges)

### Character Variations

You need to illustrate the brand character in these contexts:

#### 1. **Welcome/Hero Illustration**
- Size: Full-screen (mobile: 240px height)
- Pose: Standing, arms slightly open (welcoming)
- Expression: Warm smile, direct eye contact
- Context: Could be in a comfortable bedroom/closet setting (subtle background)
- Mood: Inviting, supportive

**Description:**
"Adora stands in a welcoming pose in what appears to be a cozy bedroom or closet space. She's smiling warmly, wearing a comfortable outfit in pastel tones. The background is simple but suggests a safe, personal space. She looks like someone you can trust."

---

#### 2. **Onboarding Step Illustrations**
- Size: Medium (150-180px)
- Pose: Different for each step
- Step 1 (Body Analysis): Thoughtful pose, hand on chin, open expression
- Step 2 (Style Preferences): Trying different outfits, confident pose
- Step 3 (Color Selection): Surrounded by color options, excited
- Step 4 (Summary): Celebrating, arms up, joyful

**Description for Step 1:**
"Adora is shown in a thoughtful but positive posture, perhaps with a hand to her chin, surrounded by soft color options. The illustration should feel exploratory and non-judgmental—like she's curious, not critical."

---

#### 3. **Empty State Illustrations**
- Size: Medium (200px)
- Scenarios:
  - No outfits created: Adora looking at empty closet, hopeful expression
  - No wardrobe items: Adora with camera, ready to help upload
  - Loading state: Adora with sparkles/magic around her (thinking, working)
  - Success celebration: Adora with arms up, confetti, huge smile

**Description for Empty Wardrobe:**
"Adora is shown holding a phone/camera, with an encouraging expression. She's in a casual outfit. The illustration feels supportive—like 'Let's fill your wardrobe together!'"

---

#### 4. **Error/Support Illustrations**
- Size: Medium (150-180px)
- Scenarios:
  - Connection error: Adora looking at phone, thoughtful but not stressed
  - Help/FAQ: Adora with lightbulb or question mark, friendly
  - Support: Adora with a caring expression, ready to help

**Description for Connection Error:**
"Adora is shown calmly looking at her phone with a slight confused but not worried expression. The tone should be 'This happens, but we'll fix it.'"

## PART 2: VIRTUAL TRY-ON PHOTOGRAPHY (For Outfit Results)

### Photography Approach: Two Options

---

## OPTION A: AI-Generated Virtual Try-On (Recommended for MVP)

### Platform Recommendation

**Best Options:**
1. **Runway ML** (generative AI + custom training)
   - Pros: Fast, scalable, customizable
   - Cons: Needs training on diverse body types
   - Cost: $100-500/month depending on volume

2. **Stable Diffusion + Custom Model** (open-source option)
   - Pros: Full control, privacy, cost-effective
   - Cons: Requires ML expertise
   - Cost: Only compute costs (~$10-50/month on cloud)

3. **CLO 3D** (professional fashion 3D)
   - Pros: Most realistic, widely used in fashion
   - Cons: Expensive, requires operator expertise
   - Cost: $500+/month

4. **Partnership with Fashion Tech Startup**
   - Options: Fit AI, Else, Superdimensional
   - Pros: Integrated solution, dedicated support
   - Cons: Revenue share or licensing fees

### Photography Style for Training Data (If using Runway/Stable Diffusion)

If you need to train a model with diverse bodies, you'll need quality training images:

**Photo Session Requirements:**

**Diversity Requirements:**
- Body types: 10+ different body shapes (pear, rectangle, hourglass, apple, etc.)
- Skin tones: 8+ different skin tones (from light to very dark)
- Ages: 20-60 age range represented
- Gender: All genders represented (trans, non-binary included)
- Clothing varieties: Same outfits on different bodies to train consistency

**Photography Specifications:**
- **Lighting:** Soft, flattering (golden hour or studio setup)
- **Background:** Pure white or light grey (for easy masking)
- **Angles:** Front, 3/4 left, 3/4 right, side (for 360° capability)
- **Clothing:** 20-30 different outfits in various styles
- **Resolution:** 2000+ pixels width (minimum)

**Each photo should:**
- Show full body (head to feet)
- Have clear arm/leg definition (no overlapping clothing)
- Show natural standing posture (not rigid)
- Have well-lit face with neutral expression
- Allow clear visibility of outfit details

### Virtual Try-On Output Style

**What the app shows users:**

**Visual Style:**
- Photorealistic but stylized (not uncanny valley)
- Smooth, flattering skin tones
- Clear, visible clothing details
- Natural body contours (respectful representation)
- Soft, professional lighting
- Clean background (white or subtle gradient)

**Body Diversity in Results:**
- **Requirement:** Show outfits on 5-8 different body types per outfit
- **User flow:** User selects their body type, sees outfit on that specific type
- **Communication:** "Este look en TU cuerpo" (This look on YOUR body)

**Image Specifications:**
- Size: 1080x1440px (9:12 aspect ratio, optimal for mobile)
- Format: JPEG (web-optimized)
- File size: <300KB per image
- Quality: 85-90% compression (balance quality + load time)

## OPTION B: Real Model Photography (For Premium/Future Version)

If you want real photos instead of AI-generated:

### Shooting Requirements

**Model Selection:**
- Hire 8-12 diverse models representing different body types
- Include: Plus-size, petite, tall, curvy, athletic, flat-chested, etc.
- Include all skin tones, ages 20-60
- Include different genders (cis and trans)

**Wardrobe:**
- 30-50 outfits in various styles (casual, professional, date, etc.)
- Mix of color palettes (align with app's color system)
- Include options in different sizes (XS-XXL on different models)

**Shooting Setup:**
- Location: Studio (white/grey background)
- Lighting: 3-point professional setup (key + fill + back light)
- Duration: 2-3 days of shooting
- Cost estimate: $3,000-8,000 per shoot day (models + crew)

**Post-Production:**
- Professional retouching (light, even-handed)
- Color correction to match app palette
- Cropping to standard sizes
- Organizing by outfit + body type

### Photography Style: Visual Direction

**Aesthetic:**
- **Lighting:** Soft, flattering, golden hour quality
- **Posture:** Natural standing, relaxed shoulders, confident stance
- **Expression:** Neutral to subtle smile (not forced)
- **Background:** Clean white or very light grey (no distractions)
- **Clothing detail:** All seams, fabric, fit details clearly visible
- **Diversity:** Celebration of different bodies—all equal, all beautiful

**What to AVOID:**
- ✗ Over-retouching (creates unrealistic standards)
- ✗ Posing that's too "model-like" (not aspirational)
- ✗ Harsh shadows or lighting (unflattering)
- ✗ Heavy makeup or styling (not relatable)
- ✗ Visible effort to "hide" body traits (shame-inducing)

**What to INCLUDE:**
- ✓ Genuine, relaxed posture
- ✓ Natural skin appearance (real pores, slight texture)
- ✓ Authentic expressions (slight smile, direct gaze)
- ✓ Equal lighting quality for all body types
- ✓ Clear visibility of outfit construction
